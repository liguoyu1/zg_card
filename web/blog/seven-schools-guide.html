All 7 Schools of Warring States Card Battles Explained

W
By the Warring States Team · Game Guide
Aug 23, 2026 · 8 min read

In Warring States: Card Battles, your school of thought is your entire identity. Each of the seven schools plays like a completely different deck — and choosing well matters more than any single card. Here is everything a beginner needs to pick their philosophy.

01How a school shapes your game

There are seven exclusive schools plus one Neutral pool shared by everyone. Every deck is built around one hero from a school, and that hero's power sets the tempo of the match. Your school decides whether you rush the opponent down, grind them out, or dismantle their board card by card.

Below, each school comes with its best beginner heroes, the playstyle it rewards, and the matchups to watch out for.

Military (兵家)

Aggro · Face · Tempo

The Military school is pure aggression. Strike fast, strike hard, and end the game before your opponent's slower plan comes together. In Warring States terms, this is the rush / aggro philosophy.

Sun Bin+2 Armor
Wu Qi+1/+1 to a friendly
Lian Po+5 Armor
Pros
  • Fast matches
  • Punishes slow decks
  • Simple, decisive plays
Cons
  • Runs out of steam
  • Weak to heavy armor

Great for: players who want quick, aggressive games.

Legalist (法家)

Control · Disruption · Silence

The Legalist school enforces order. It silences, bounces, and manages the board with precision — punishing anyone who builds a fancy combo by stripping it away.

Shang Yang-2 cost to biggest minion
Han FeiSilence an enemy
Shen BuhaiBounce an enemy
Pros
  • Shuts down combos
  • Strong mid-game
Cons
  • Slower start
  • Needs planning

Great for: thinking players who like controlling the flow.

Confucian (儒家)

Value · Buff · Sustain

The Confucian school grows steadily. Draw, heal, and buff to out-value the opponent over time. It rewards patience and a strong late game.

ConfuciusDraw a card
MenciusHeal 3
Xunzi+2/+2 to a friendly
Pros
  • Great card advantage
  • Excellent late game
Cons
  • Slow to start
  • Vulnerable to early rush

Great for: patient builders who enjoy outlasting opponents.

Taoist (道家)

Defense · Immunity · Board-wide

The Taoist school flows with nature. It protects its board with immunity and armor, and builds a wide, resilient army that is hard to remove.

Laozi+1/+1 to all friendly
ZhuangziGive immunity
LieziGive Charge
Pros
  • Very hard to remove
  • Strong board-wide buffs
Cons
  • Less direct damage

Great for: defensive players who love an unkillable board.

Mohist (墨家)

Siege · Summon · Weapon

The Mohist school builds machinery and overwhelming armies. Summon constructs, strengthen weapons, and batter down defenses with engineered precision.

MoziSummon a 1/1 construct
Gongshu Ban+1 weapon attack
Qin Huali+3 Armor
Pros
  • Wide, sticky board
  • Powerful weapons
Cons
  • Relies on setup

Great for: engineers at heart who enjoy building up a board.

Yinyang (阴阳家)

Magic · Discovery · Random

The Yinyang school weaves the five elements. Random damage, card discovery, and elemental spells make every match slightly different — high ceiling, high variance.

Zou YanRandom 3 damage
Gan DeDiscover a spell
Shi ShenDraw + copy spells
Pros
  • Huge spell burst
  • Unpredictable to play against
Cons
  • Relies on luck
  • Inconsistent

Great for: players who love spells, discovery, and a little chaos.

Diplomat (纵横家)

Disruption · Draw · Random

The Diplomat school is all about alliances and breaking them. Weaken entire enemy boards, draw aggressively, and keep opponents guessing.

Su Qin-1/-1 to all enemies
Zhang YiDraw two cards
GuiguziRandom two effects
Pros
  • Great board-wide disruption
  • Strong card draw
Cons
  • Some randomness

Great for: strategists who like weakening everyone at once.

02Which school should you pick?

New to the game? Start with Military — it teaches the core loop quickly. Want something calmer? Confucian rewards steady play. Love disruption? Legalist or Diplomat will feel like home. The best first step is simply trying a few heroes in casual matches until one clicks.
