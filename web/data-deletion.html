Data Deletion Request

App: Warring States Card (package: WSCard / 战国卡牌)　|　Last updated: 2026-11-17　|　Contact: zgfylgy@163.com

This page explains how users of Warring States Card can request deletion of their data. You can request deletion of some or all of your data without deleting your account, or permanently delete the entire account and all associated data.

1. Delete specific data while keeping your account

If you want to keep your account but remove certain stored data, send us a deletion request by email:

  • To: zgfylgy@163.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request
  • From: the email address registered with your account (so we can verify your identity).
  • Include: your registered nickname / player name, and clearly state which data you want deleted (for example: match history, ranking records, saved decks, or other specific records).

After we verify your identity, we process the request within a reasonable time, typically within 30 days. Your account and login remain active unless you ask for full account deletion.

2. Data that can be deleted on request

  • Account profile: nickname, avatar, profile details linked to your account.
  • Game records: match history, battle records, ranking records, saved decks and card collections you request to remove.
  • Game progress & balance: progress records, gold and gem balances, unlocked items and assets you request to clear.
  • Guest data on this device: clear the app cache, or uninstall the app, to remove a local guest account and its local progress.

3. Data that may be retained

  • If you keep your account, your login identifier and credentials remain so you can keep using the service.
  • Minimal transaction / order records, payment receipts and anti-fraud / security records may be retained for the shortest period required by law, accounting, tax or dispute-resolution obligations.
  • Backups are purged on their regular rotation schedule; retained legal records are deleted once the mandatory retention period ends.

4. Delete the entire account and all data

To permanently remove the account and every associated personal data:

  • In the app: open the account menu on the home screen and choose Delete Account, then confirm twice. The cloud account, profile, game progress, rankings, in-game currency and items are permanently removed.
  • By email: send a message to zgfylgy@163.com with the subject Delete Account, from your registered email, describing your request. We verify and complete the deletion within a reasonable time, typically 30 days.

Deleted account data cannot be recovered; in-game currency and items are forfeited. Legal records described in section 3 are retained only as required by law.

5. Guest accounts

Guest (unregistered) data is stored on the device and tied to a guest identifier. Clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app deletes local guest data. Contact us by email if a guest-linked record still appears after reinstalling.

数据删除申请（中文）

应用：战国卡牌 Warring States Card（WSCard）　|　最后更新：2026-11-17　|　联系邮箱：zgfylgy@163.com

本页面说明《战国卡牌》用户如何申请删除个人数据。你可以在保留账号的前提下申请删除部分或全部数据，也可以彻底删除整个账号及相关数据。

一、保留账号，删除指定数据

如希望保留账号但清除部分已存储的数据，请通过邮件提交删除申请：

  • 收件人：zgfylgy@163.com
  • 邮件标题：数据删除申请
  • 发件邮箱：请使用账号注册邮箱发送，以便核验身份。
  • 邮件内容：注明注册昵称 / 玩家名，并明确说明需要删除哪些数据（例如：对战记录、排行记录、保存的卡组或其他指定记录）。

身份核验通过后，我们将在合理期限内处理，通常不超过 30 天。除非你申请删除整个账号，否则账号与登录状态保持有效。

二、可应申请删除的数据

  • 账号资料：昵称、头像及与账号关联的个人资料。
  • 游戏记录：对战记录、战绩、排行记录、你要求清除的卡组与卡牌收藏。
  • 进度与余额：进度记录、金币与钻石余额、你要求清空的已解锁道具与资产。
  • 本机游客数据：清除应用缓存或卸载应用，即可删除本机游客账号及其本地进度。

三、可能保留的数据

  • 保留账号的情况下，登录标识与凭证会继续保留，以便你正常使用服务。
  • 为遵守法律法规、满足财务税务、交易争议处理或风控反欺诈要求，少量交易 / 订单记录、支付凭证与安全风控记录将按法律要求的最短期限保留。
  • 备份数据按常规轮换周期清除；法定保留期满后，上述留存记录一并删除。

四、删除整个账号及全部数据

如需永久删除账号及全部个人数据，可通过以下任一方式：

  • 应用内：在首页账号菜单中选择「删除账号」，并两次确认。云端账号、资料、游戏进度、排行、游戏内货币与道具将被永久清除。
  • 邮件：从注册邮箱发送邮件至 zgfylgy@163.com，标题注明「删除账号」并说明诉求。我们将核验后在合理期限内（通常 30 天内）完成删除。

账号删除后数据不可恢复，游戏内货币与道具一并清除；第三条所述法定记录仅在法律要求范围内保留。

五、游客账号

游客（未注册）数据存储于本机并与游客标识绑定。清除应用缓存或卸载应用即可删除本机游客数据；若重装后仍出现游客关联记录，请邮件联系我们处理。
