What is Warring States Card?

Warring States: Card Battles is a free, instant-to-play card battle game. It turns the Hundred Schools of Thought of China's Warring States period into playable card archetypes: Military, Legalist, Confucian, Daoist, Mohist, Yin-Yang, and Strategist — each a distinct way to play. Play instantly in your browser or download the client and battle anywhere.
